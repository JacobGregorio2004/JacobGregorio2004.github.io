Need help with your instrument purchase? We got you covered!

This guide was made with the intention of buying either yourself or your child's first instrument. With that being said, it is never too late to pick up a new hobby or skill! This guide can be used for any and all ages. Good luck on the adventure you are about to embark on!

1. Plan a Budget Beforehand!

In a perfect world, instruments would be free for everyone! But unfortunately, in real life, instruments can be quite expensive, and it's recommended that you plan out a budget beforehand so that you don't make any irrational purchases. Instruments vary in price due to materials and the labor required to make them. For a first instrument (and especially for a child) it is normally best to go with a cheaper/used option. BUT DO NOT BE FOOLED, just because an instrument looks sufficient does not mean that it is good. For example, there are trumpets on amazon for around $100-$200 dollars. They might look good in the pictures, but I promise they are not of good quality. The only exception I could see with purchasing instruments that low in quality is for a very young child, or perhaps someone who plans to just use it for decoration.


2. Where to buy from?

The next step in buying your first instrument is to know where to look. You have two options: purchasing online or going to a music store. With online purchasing the pros include a wider range of instrument options, cheaper prices (if buying used), and if you're shipping to your house, you save gas money! Cons include not being able to try out the instrument before buying and possibly even being scammed online if you buy from an unreliable source. With in-person purchases, pros include being able to try out the instrument and helpful guidance from the in-person workers. Cons include prices being generally more, even on used products (similar to how car purchasing works), other fees might be added on as well such as insurance for the instrument (if you choose to or if required due to payment plans).


3. Do Your Reasearch!

Make sure to research different brands of instruments because they do have many differences. There is no perfect brand. Lots of manufacturers make fantastic products but there is one aspect that determines your choice the most, your preference! Yes, anyone can play on any instrument, but many prefer to stick to what is most comfortable for them. Even the best products out there (that many people use) are rejected by players just because the mouthpiece resistance might be too much, the instrument might be too heavy to carry around, or even them just not liking how it feels in their hands. Many professionals tend to stick to one brand just because it is what they play on best and what they find most comfortable. So again, DO YOUR RESEARCH!!
