Description:

The flute is a woodwind instrument known for its light, clear, and bright sound. It is commonly used in concert bands, orchestras, chamber groups, and solo music. The main parts of a flute include the headjoint, lip plate, body, keys, footjoint, and tone holes. Unlike many other woodwind instruments, the flute does not use a reed, and its long metal body gives it a very distinct appearance.

How to Play the Flute:

The flute is played by blowing air across the opening in the lip plate. The player presses different combinations of keys to open and close tone holes, which changes the notes that are produced. Notes can also be changed by adjusting airflow, lip position, and the angle of the air stream.
