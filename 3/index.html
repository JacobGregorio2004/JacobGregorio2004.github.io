Project 2: Web Application Proposal

Topic Description:

  1. I selected the topic “The Orchestra (Music Theory)” with musical instruments specifically in mind. I wish to educate others on a plethora of different instruments and to show people how interesting and intricate they are. The reason why this topic is of interest to me is because I have been associated with many different musical organizations from middle school to now with the University of Mississippi's musical programs. I believe my web application will help because I know a decent number of people who can't even differentiate between two well known instruments and connecting an image to a description would help them out a fair bit. Another reason it could help is by giving more insight/information to middle schoolers so it can help them choose their main instrument.

  2. There were a few examples that I found online that sort of solved the problem I was looking to solve. The first one I came across was from an orchestra named “Philharmonia” that showed only the instruments they used, but included pictures, descriptions, and even videos with more information(1). The second one is called “Instruments of the world Virtual Encyclopedia” and has many different instruments, even some that I have not seen before so this would be too overwhelming for beginners(2). The last example I found was made by Yamaha called the “Musical Instrument Guide”. It contained a fair amount of more well-known instruments and under each instrument they also gave the most information including a short description, origins, structure, how to play, how the instrument is made, choosing an instrument, and care and maintenance. The third example is honestly a really good web application but could still be too much for beginner players(3).

App Description:

  1. On the landing page, I plan to have a short introduction to the user in order to describe what the website is all about and maybe some basics on how to use it. I plan on having images on the different categories of instruments along with text saying the specific category.

  2. The first four choices are Woodwinds, Brass, Strings, and Percussion. Under Woodwinds will be flute, clarinet, saxophone, oboe. Under Brass will be trumpet, french horn, trombone, tuba. Under Strings will be violin, viola, cello, and double bass. Under Percusion will be snare drum, mallet percussion, cymbals, and bass drum. The final information will contain: One basic image, one more in-depth image, description of the instrument, and information on how the instrument is played.

  3. The third page will consist of a guide on how to go about buying the instrument.

  4. The “Contact Us” page will say something like “Wanna talk about your favorite musical instruments? Send an email!” I intend on getting their email address for comunication purposes.
