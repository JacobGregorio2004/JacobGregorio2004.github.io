Thorfinn

Short Description:

This is the main character "Thorfinn" from the anime called Vinland Saga!
Throughout the anime, we see Thorfinn experience tragedy after tragedy and grows up while in war.
His main goal in life is to become a true warrior and live up to his father's name.

Reasons I Like This Character:

  • He is a strong and skillful character
  • Despite the fact that he went through so much and did so much wrong while in war, he still tries to become a better person.
  • He is self aware of his flaws and actively tries to be better because of that.
